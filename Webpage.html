Computers in Healthcare


Computers can be used in healthcare to help with a number of tasks - some of these tasks include surgery, medical imaging, diagnosis, administration, and managing patient records.


Telemedicine

Computers have allowed people to receive medical advice and diagnosis from their own homes, as well as having medicine delivered to them. This is beneficial to many people who aren't able to attend a typical, in-person appointment, and helps prevent people from not getting illnesses diagnosed due to feeling like the inconvenience of travelling to a doctor outweighs their concerns.
